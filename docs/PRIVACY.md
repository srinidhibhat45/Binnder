# Binnder Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

Binnder is an Android app that helps you review the files on your phone's shared storage and decide whether to keep them, move them to a recycle bin, or delete them.

## What Binnder accesses

- **Files on shared storage.** Binnder asks for Android's "All files access" permission so it can list, preview, move, restore, and delete files you review. This access is used only to perform the actions you choose in the app.
- **App-private records.** Binnder stores a small on-device database of files you have kept, files in its recycle bin, and pending operations, so these survive app restarts.

## What Binnder does not do

- It does not upload, share, or sell your files or any information about them.
- It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.
- The Google Play version of Binnder does not request the Internet permission and does not send any data off your device.

## Data retention and deletion

All data stays on your device. Binnder's recycle bin keeps moved files for 30 days in a `.Binnder-trash` folder on the same storage volume, then deletes them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes Binnder's private records; restore any binned files you want to keep before uninstalling.

## Children

Binnder is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any personal information.
